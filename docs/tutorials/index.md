# Tutorials

A tutorial is not a story and not a sales pitch. It is a teacher: it takes one topic and treats
it on its own terms, with the details, the contracts, and the traps that a short example has to
leave out. Read these when you have time to spend and you want to understand — or change — how the
library works, not just what it does.

| Tutorial | It teaches | Time budget |
|---|---|---|
| [Getting Started](getting-started.md) | Install, run, and put a predictor on your own Gymnasium environment, including the pitfalls | an hour |
| [A new family and its DRPP](developer-guide.md) | A new ambiguity-set family and the DRPP it needs, end to end | a day |
| [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) | The traps, what they look like when they bite, and what to do about them | as needed |
| [The map](map.md) | How the pages and the package fit together, for reading in any order | ten minutes |

The last one is also the answer to “where does this fit?” when the shelf order does not match your
question. The full route through the material, including the experiments and the papers, is the
[map](map.md).
